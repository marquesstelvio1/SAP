# Security Skill

## Scope

Threat modeling, authorization, data classification, export controls, secrets, audit, and compliance readiness.

## Rules

- No feature without permission and audit impact.
- No export without classification and retention controls.
- No service credential without rotation and scope.
- No platform support access without approval and audit.

## Outputs

- Threat notes.
- Permission impact.
- Audit requirements.
- Required security tests.
